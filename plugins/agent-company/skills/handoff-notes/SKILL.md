---
# Handoff note

```
HANDOFF → <next agent>
Done: <one line>
Files / outputs: <paths or "inline above">
Needs from you: <one line>
Watch out for: <one line, or "nothing">
```
Keep it under 60 words. No recap of the whole conversation.
