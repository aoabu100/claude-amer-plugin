# Research brief

```
## Question
## Findings  (bullets, each with a source link or "unverified")
## Options compared  (table, if the task is a choice)
## Confidence  (high / medium / low, with why)
## Open questions
```
- Prefer primary sources (official docs, original reports).
- Quote at most one short phrase per source; paraphrase the rest.
- Date every fact that can change (prices, versions, people in roles).
